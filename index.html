Every B2B buyer is one of these four.

We score every contact on DISC — the framework already in the bones of Sandler, Tracom Social Styles, and most modern sales training. Built for live use across email, call, and meeting.

It's behavioral and observable. A rep can spot it in two emails or the first 90 seconds of a call — and act on it without leaving the conversation.

What we add

We layer MBTI as a decision-style overlay where it adds signal, and The Challenger Customer's stakeholder roles to read the whole buying committee — not just the contact.

Why it's different

Most tools score activity. RevSage scores people — and gives reps the next sentence, the next subject line, the next move.

For the CRO who's seen this movie before

The questions you'd ask on a real call.

How is this different from Outreach AI, Salesloft Rhythm, or Gong's Forecast?
Those tools optimize the activity — sequences, calls, score cards. RevSage scores the person. We sit on top of your existing stack, read every contact's DISC profile and the committee around them, and tell the rep the next sentence to say. We integrate with all three; we don't replace them.
Why DISC and not a more "modern" framework?
DISC is what most B2B sales orgs already speak — Sandler, Tracom Social Styles, Dale Carnegie all map to it. It's behavioral, observable, and a rep can apply it inside a live conversation. We layer MBTI as a decision-style overlay where it adds signal, and the Challenger Customer's stakeholder roles to read the buying committee. The combination is the differentiator.
How accurate is the personality scoring?
We blend public signals (LinkedIn, content, role), CRM behavior, and your call/email exchanges. Confidence is exposed on every profile — reps see low / med / high and know when to trust it. Profiles update after every reply and every call. Most accounts cross "high confidence" inside the first three touches.
Will my reps actually use it?
They never have to leave Outreach, Salesloft, or HubSpot. The pre-call brief lands in their calendar, the message variants land in their sequencer, the post-meeting follow-up drafts itself. No new dashboard, no new tab. We measure adoption weekly and share it with you.
What about data privacy and security?
SOC 2 Type II, GDPR-aligned, AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit. Region-locked data residency (US, EU, India). We do not train shared models on your data — ever. Full subprocessor list and DPA available before you sign anything.
How fast does it deploy?
Pilot live in under a week. Connect CRM, sequencer, and call recorder via OAuth; we backfill the last 90 days; reps see profiles on day three. Most teams see win-rate movement inside 30 days.
Does it work for outbound, inbound, and expansion?
Yes — different plays per motion. Outbound emphasizes channel-tuned cold messaging. Inbound emphasizes fast committee mapping on warm leads. Expansion emphasizes who's championing internally and who's quietly blocking.
How do you price this?
Per active rep, with annual contracts. We size pricing to your team and your motion in the first call — no list price tricks. Demos are free, pilots are paid against an agreed success metric.
